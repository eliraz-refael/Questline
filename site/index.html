How a prompt is graded.

A small model scores every prompt of six words or more on seven qualities, each from 0 to 10, against what that prompt actually needs. A quick follow-up question isn't marked down for skipping context it doesn't need.

Up to 20 XP a prompt

Your XP is your average grade times two, so a perfect 10 earns 20. Prompts are where the daily XP comes from.

Quality over volume

Spamming prompts doesn't pay. The rate drops as the day goes on.

The game never sees your prompts

Grading runs inside your own Claude Code. The game server receives only the scores. Your prompt text and the grader's note never reach it.
